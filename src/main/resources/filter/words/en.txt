# English dictionary for the containing-word check.

# This is NOT a blocklist. It is what stops `assassin` and `Scunthorpe` being rejected: a match that
# is a proper substring of a known word is allowed, and a match that is the whole token is not.
#
# A STARTER, and deliberately a targeted one. A full English dictionary would be ~50k lines and most
# of it would never be consulted, because only words that *contain* a blocklist term ever reach this
# check. So what follows is exactly that set, plus the place names that famously collide.
#
# The real list comes from the Hunspell en_GB/en_US dictionaries plus a place-name gazetteer - see
# ../seed/README.md step 5. Anything missing here fails closed: the block stands, the user disputes
# it from the rejection dialog, and a moderator allowlists it in one click.
#
# The hazard to watch when adding to this file: a word here that *contains* a blocklist term becomes
# an evasion vector unless that exact form is itself on the blocklist. `Wankdorf` and the Austrian
# village of `Fucking` are real place names and were deliberately left out for that reason.
