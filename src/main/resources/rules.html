Community rules

Comments and ratings on the Kotatsu-Redo community server.

This is a place to talk about manga. Comments publish instantly — nobody reads them before you press send — so these rules are what keeps that possible.

What belongs here

  • Talk about the work, the chapter, the art, the translation, the story.
  • Disagree freely. Say a chapter was bad, say the ending was a mistake, say you dropped it.
  • Warn people before you spoil something they have not read yet.

What gets removed

  • Slurs and attacks on people for who they are. This one is not negotiable.
  • Swearing. The bar here is higher than most places on purpose: you can say a chapter was terrible without swearing at it, and threads read better when everyone does.
  • Going after another user rather than an argument.
  • Sexual content involving minors, described in any way. Removed and banned, with no discussion.
  • Spam, advertising, and links to anything for sale.
  • Personal information about anyone, yours or someone else's.

Why your comment was rejected

A word filter runs when you press send. If it stops you, the app tells you which word — rewrite that part and try again. The filter is not clever: it matches words, not meaning, so it will occasionally be wrong about a perfectly ordinary sentence.

When that happens, tap “this was wrong”. That goes straight to the people who maintain the list, and it is the fastest way a bad rule gets fixed for everyone.

Arguments

Two people can exchange three replies in one thread and then have to stop. If someone else joins the conversation the limit no longer applies. This is not a judgement about who was right — it is there because a long two-person argument is unreadable for everybody else.

Likes and dislikes

There is no report button. Dislikes are how a weak comment sinks and how moderators find what needs looking at. Dislike things you think are bad; do not organise other people to do it.

If you are banned

A ban deletes your comments and ends your access. There is no appeal inside the app — ask on Discord, below. If you were banned by mistake, say so there.

Your data

There is no email address and no password here. Your account is a key generated on your device; if you lose it, nobody can recover it for you — which is also why nobody can tie your comments to you. Back it up. You can delete everything about yourself from the app's settings, instantly and without asking anyone.

Questions, appeals, or a rule that keeps misfiring: the Kotatsu-Redo Discord, or the issue tracker.
These rules are versioned with the server that serves them.
